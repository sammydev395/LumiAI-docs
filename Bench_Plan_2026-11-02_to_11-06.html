4. Board install order

Power bay (Halo, PSU-cover mount):

  1. Copper heatsink on the Mus-14's buck IC (the small square chip, NOT the inductor).
  2. Move the G469 one hole toward the edge. Dry-fit its harness reach before unscrewing it. Record the new holes.
  3. Mount the Mus-14 beside it: screws if it has holes, VHB if it doesn't.
  4. Main power OFF. Solder both wires at the Mus-14 pads, then land H2 (black) first. Leave H1's (red) fork unlanded and taped for now: the backfeed check (§6) runs in that state. Red fork is landed last, power off. Plug D18 into the Mus-14 only.

Wand (bench fit-up; the halves are not printed):

  1. PCA9685: bridge pad A0 (→ 0x41) FIRST, before it ever touches Halo's bus (0x40 is the HAT). Trim the top-end header, link OE→GND at the pads, then solder the 4 PCA leads.
  2. Drivers: on the first KESHY, beep every pad against the header map (1-2 VIN+ · 3-4 VIN− · 5 PWM · 6 LED+ · 7 LED−) BEFORE trimming. Then trim, keeping the 0805 pull-down. Solder DP / DW / DL per seat.
  3. Rails: build the 3 ST1 rails and meter every strip end to end. Then build the cradle board (W10), then the P and S30 pigtails.
  4. Wire in this order: rails ← drivers (DP), then the PCA (DW by channel), then TMP and the fan.

  • PCA with the header trimmed and leads on: measured size, and which end is CH0.
  • Seated 4-pin Dupont height on the PCA header (CAD allows 1.0 mm to the 0° drivers).
  • Diameter of the DL bundle (12 twisted 26 AWG pairs) going up the 5 mm annulus.
  • String-measured lengths that differ from the table.

5. Mus-14 voltage-drop test (on Halo)

Pass = HAT vbus_min ≥ 4.90 V in every state, and ≤ 5.25 V at idle. The gate binds on the minimum, not the mean.

Readings in every state:

  • V_src = the inline meter at the Mus-14 socket.
  • V_hat = the HAT INA219 vbus_min.
  • A = the INA219 amps.
  • Drop = V_src − V_hat.

Load = Halo's own HAT + SSD + OAK. There is no electronic load. Off-robot there is no loaded reading: the Mus-14 is metered with no load only.

Step State Time
T0 Off-robot: bench supply 13.8 V → Mus-14, no load. Record both sockets (expect 5.0–5.2; the Mus-14 is fixed 5 V, there is no trim). 2 min
T1 On Halo with H1 landed and D18 not in the HAT: Mus-14 socket V (5.0–5.2). 1 min
T2 Plug D18 into the HAT. Pi idle, stack running, OAK off. 3 min
T3 Cold boot from the rocker. Watch the meter's minimum. 1 boot
T4 SSD stress (below). 5 min
T5 OAK streaming, its normal MJPEG profile. 5 min
T6 Worst case today: OAK streaming + SSD stress (the script's default, no mic). 10 min

What a pass proves: the Mus-14 + HOTNOW hold Halo's real load as connected tomorrow. What it doesn't prove:

  • Without the stick, ~0.5 A of the 2.6 A design peak is missing, so re-run T6 at the speaker phase.
  • It is not the 20-boot soak (BC-3); that comes later.

Fail action, in order:

  1. Retest with a 5 A e-marked cable if the drop is the problem.
  2. The LM25116 (CV 5.20 V) replaces the Mus-14. ⚠️ It is NOT ordered (Purchasing 2026-11-02), so a fail here means ordering it.
  3. Load-shed: the new mic goes on a Pi native port.

6. Backfeed check, direction (b) — 2 min, not skippable

The Pi is ON and the Mus-14 is OFF, with D18 still in the HAT. With no F4, "Mus-14 OFF" = H1's red fork unlanded and taped (the rocker can't do it: it cuts the Pi too).

  1. Meter the Mus-14 output (the meter at its socket) and its input terminals. Pass ≈ 0 V. Anything ≥ ~0.5 V means the HAT is back-driving the buck: unplug D18 and stop.
  2. Expect the HAT to keep running from the Pi's 5 V, because that is how it runs today.
    • The check that matters is the path check (BC-4): run SSD stress with H1 unlanded, then (power off, land H1, power on) with H1 landed, and watch the Pi's input current (2nd meter, Q3).
    • With H1 landed, the Pi's current must drop by roughly the HAT current. If it doesn't, the HAT is still riding the Pi rail. Stop and report.

7. Safety and power-up order

  1. Before touching the DROK terminals:
    • Arm at HOME and supported. Clean shutdown (sudo poweroff).
    • DC rocker OFF. Then meter DROK input +V = 0 V.
    • The APIELE stays live on the UPS battery even with AC off: no tools on the APIELE.
  2. Before the first power-on, meter with everything still off:
    • Mus-14 IN+ to IN−: not a short (> 1 kΩ).
    • Polarity: red goes to IN+.
    • No strand bridging at any fork.
    • Tug-test every crimp.
  3. First power-on with the Mus-14 fed: H1 landed, D18 not in the HAT, rocker ON. Meter the socket at 5.0–5.2 V, then plug in D18 (step T2).
  4. Wand: meter VIN+ to VIN− on each rail before any driver is plugged in. First power comes from a current-limited bench supply, not the tether.
