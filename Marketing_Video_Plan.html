The fleet is not being migrated — Jazzy is a sim island that records from / can drive the Humble robots.

4. Marketing video pipeline — two-look from one trajectory

Decision: author motion once, render twice.

URDF + meshes (already validated by ros2_control)
        │
   joint trajectory   ← teleop-record real hardware → rosbag
        │
   ┌────┴────┐
   ▼         ▼
GAZEBO     BLENDER
realistic   cinematic
physics     Cycles
mp4 record  AMD HIP
            studio lighting

Why two looks:

  • Gazebo Harmonic (Gazebo Sim 8) — already installed via ROS 2 Jazzy. Renders are "engineering-grade" — good enough for technical/demo audiences, has real physics. Native video capture via gz sim (GUI VideoRecorder plugin) → mp4. Fast iteration.
  • Blender 4.x + Phobos URDF importer — Cycles renders are cinematic. Full control over lighting, camera, depth of field, motion blur. AMD HIP works on the Radeon 8060S. Slower per-shot but worth it for hero footage.
  • Cut both together in DaVinci Resolve (free on Linux).

Trajectory authoring decision: teleoperate real hardware, record /joint_states + /cmd_vel to a rosbag. Hand-authored joint angles look mechanical; MoveIt-planned trajectories look "engineering correct" but not alive. Teleop motion has natural rhythm — much better for marketing. Replay the same bag in both Gazebo and Blender.

Why not Isaac Sim: photoreal but NVIDIA RTX only. Radeon 8060S not supported.

6. Environment + camera plan

6.1 Environment — clean studio (white infinity backdrop)

Pure white "cyc" floor curving up into a white wall. Like an Apple product reveal.

Why: fastest to set up, no environment competing with the robot, works identically in Gazebo (white ambient + bright key+fill lights) and Blender (HDRI studio + white floor). Themed environments (workbench/lab, modern living room) deferred until the pipeline is proven.

6.2 Camera moves — 4-shot starter pack (~25-30 sec total)

Shot Duration Camera Purpose
Opening 3-5 s Slow dolly-in, front-low → 3/4 hero angle "Reveal" — car-ad style
Action wide 10-15 s Locked-off 3/4 wide, no camera move Robot moves across frame, camera doesn't compete
Feature close-up 3-5 s Slow push-in + rack focus from OAK-D camera → BigClaw + Wand "Engineering" beat
Closing orbit 5-8 s Slow 360° orbit at ~30° elevation, ends where it started Recap; reusable as website hero loop

Principles applied:

  1. Camera and subject shouldn't both move fast. When the robot does its main action, the camera is locked. When the camera moves, the robot holds a pose or does something subtle.
  2. Every shot has one clear "read." Opening = reveal. Action wide = motion. Close-up = detail. Closing = recap.

A. Cross-distro DDS — validated architecture (Jazzy host ↔︎ Humble fleet)

Validated on the EVO X2 (sammydev295-NucBox-EVO-X2) 2026-06-10. The sim/render host runs ROS 2 Jazzy; the robot fleet runs ROS 2 Humble in Docker. They communicate directly, peer-to-peer over DDS — there is no broker and no relay container.

Topology

  Ultra      (Humble/Docker, raspberrypistage, --network host) ─┐
  Rosmaster  (Humble/Docker, jetsonnanodev,    --network host) ─┼─ DDS domain 100, CycloneDDS ─► Jazzy host
                                                                 │                                (Gazebo / MoveIt2 / Blender)
              all on 192.168.1.0/24 — flat LAN, no DDS router/bridge needed
  • The robots talk to the Jazzy host directly. The local ros2_control_plane Humble container on the EVO X2 is not a bridge and not in the data path — it was only a local Humble stand-in used during validation.
  • DDS discovery is automatic for every participant sharing ROS_DOMAIN_ID=100 + RMW_IMPLEMENTATION=rmw_cyclonedds_cpp that can route to each other on the LAN.

What was tested

Test Result
Humble (Docker) → Jazzy host, std_msgs/String ✅ received
Jazzy host discovers full live fleet (/joint_states, /tf, /robot_description, /depth_cam/*, /rosmaster_oak/*) ✅
Real fleet standard msg → Jazzy host (/rosmaster_oak/rgb/camera_info, 1280×720) ✅ decoded cleanly
Jazzy host → Humble (Docker), reverse direction, unpinned ❌ discovery OK, data did not deliver
Jazzy host → Humble (Docker), reverse direction, NIC-pinned ✅ received

Gotcha + required fix: pin the NIC

The EVO X2 has two NICs on the same subnet (eno1 192.168.1.33 + wlp195s0 192.168.1.84) plus docker bridges. CycloneDDS auto-selects one interface per participant, which caused asymmetric data delivery (robots→host worked, host→robots silently dropped despite discovery succeeding). Fix — pin the wired NIC on every participant (add to .bashrc next to the RMW/domain lines, and confirm the robots pin their wired NIC too):

export CYCLONEDDS_URI='<CycloneDDS><Domain><General><Interfaces><NetworkInterface name="eno1"/></Interfaces></General></Domain></CycloneDDS>'

Robots→host (the record direction) works even unpinned, but pinning is required for host→robot (MoveIt2 driving the real arm, or replaying a recorded trajectory back to hardware) and makes everything deterministic.

Implication for the pipeline

Live DDS to the robots is only needed for the record step (teleop → MCAP rosbag of /joint_states + /cmd_vel). The render step replays the bag locally into Gazebo/MoveIt/Blender — robots can be offline. Record direct, then work from the bag.

Benign noise: Humble↔︎Jazzy logs Failed to parse type hash ... from USER_DATA '(null)' warnings on first discovery (Humble doesn't advertise the RIHS type hash Jazzy expects). Data flows correctly regardless; the warnings are a one-time discovery transient, not a per-message cost.
