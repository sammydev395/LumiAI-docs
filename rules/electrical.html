Electrical rules — the chain from PSU to LED

Back to ../../CLAUDE.md

  • 🔴 The STM32 DROK setpoint IS the arm's servo voltage: 7.5 V, never above 8.4 V (operator, 2026-11-02). The STM32 board passes its input straight to the bus-servo ports; the board, the LX-15D and the HTS-25L all top out at 8.4 V. The old 9 V setpoint was justified against the board alone and ran Halo's servos at 9.2 V. 7.5 V = Hiwonder's own adapter for this board.
  • Wire palette is LOCKED: 18 / 22 / 26 AWG (operator, 2026-09-30, inventory standardization). 18 = power (every bay 13.8 V segment), 22 = mid (tether V+/GND, fan branches, taps), 26 = signal (I²C, 3V3, pull-ups, LED bundle). Ferrules E1008 / E0508 / E0208. The v1 as-built differs (16 AWG bay runs, 24 AWG G469 landings) and stays — label as-built vs new-build, never silently renumber a gauge.
  • ⛔ Never tin a wire end into a screw terminal. Solder cold-flows under clamp pressure and the joint backs out months later as an intermittent fault (2026-08-14 rule). Ferrule or bare stranded.
  • The G469 ships with NO terminal silkscreen — the product photos show one; the board is blank. Operator-applied labels per docs/images/electronics_bay/g469_bay_labels.png. Bench-proven ferrules in its 2.54 mm cages: E0308 (24 AWG), E0208 (26 AWG), and the AIRIC violet ferrule for 22 AWG or multi-conductor (proven to 0.516 mm² — four 26 AWG bench-crimped + as-built on consecutive terminals, 2026-09-23). The XHF kit's E0508 is ⛔ banned at the G469 (2026-09-01 bench test — does not fit the clamps). ⛔ *Until 2026-10-11 this line called 22 AWG "E0508, unproven — verify or use 26"; retracted on operator challenge — the AIRIC part was the proven path all along. A partially-seated G469 = empty i2cdetect + reboot loops — reseat before any other diagnosis.
  • The wand star ground is G469 pin 9, only. GND-logic never lands on the distribution block — two grounds on two already-tied bay nodes is a parallel return that diverts ~0.85 A of LED current through the Pi. The rail GND bus cut plus the single pin-9 meeting point IS the design (2026-09-23).
  • The bay-fan signal-GND jumper (→ G469 pin 34) is ON HOLD, not installed (2026-09-30) — do not build it from older text.
  • Distribution block: the modified APIELE is current-generation (Halo as-built + the pending Ultra retrofit, 4 spares in stock); the D-1459 / HL-PBB takes over from the Nova build. Port maps: Power_Bay_Design.md §11.3.3 (D-1459) and §11.3.7 (APIELE, collapsed).
  • 🔴 The LED drivers are XL4001E-class bucks, NOT PT4115. Two mistakes already made and withdrawn: the PT4115 dimming model (">2.5 V = 100 %, 0.5–2.5 V analog, <0.3 V shutdown") is wrong for this hardware, and the generic XL4001E datasheet V_REF (≈0.235 V) predicts 1.06 A against the real 0.222 Ω sense — also wrong. Measured wins: V_REF ≈ 0.155 V, R = 0.222 Ω → 700 mA; PWM is active-HIGH. Loupe-verify each board's IC silk + sense network per the build plan §2.1 before trusting any current figure. The PT4115 mistake cost several days of shipping in India.
  • The driver's dim input is a hard ENABLE, not an analog dimmer — measured 2026-09-03: full current at 1.67 V, zero at 0.828 V, no analog region. An RC filter or DAC into that pin is a comparator, not a dimmer. ⛔ INVRT=0. Both vendor listings claim active-LOW and both are wrong; INVRT=1 boots all 12 channels full-on.
  • The PCA9685 tops out near 1.6 kHz, so 1 kHz PWM banding on camera is fixed by a different PWM source, never by a config change or by going analog — analog dimming moves each die's chromaticity with current and breaks an RGBW colour mix. See creator/docs/Creator_Dimming.md.
  • Doc ownership (three-way split, 2026-09-29): bay electrics live in Power_Bay_Design.md; general bench work (inventory §1, crimping §3, LED MCPCB §12, bonding §15) in Electronics_Assembly_Guide.md; the wand bench appendix is Lumi_Wand_Integration.md §19a–§25. Section moves preserve anchors 1:1. After editing anything they guard, run docs/images/check_figures_match_docs.py and docs/images/electronics_bay/check_g469_map.py — both must end green.
  • Epoxy day is gated twice (one-way bond): Bench Phase 3 must pass AND the E1/E2 emitter + optic evaluation must be complete — E1/E2 picks what gets bonded.
  • ⛔ Never push-score stripboard with a scalpel to size a board (operator safety call, 2026-10-06). Board-to-size = the scroll saw (in hand): cut just outside a hole row, file back; fine skip-tooth blade, tape the cut line, dust mask (FR4 dust + a recirculating garage fan). Track mods = spot-face cutter / 3 mm drill by hand. The scalpel is for SMD trace cuts on flat boards only.
  • Cartridge plate joints (v3.2 / 6053128, 2026-10-09): pack leads 18 AWG silicone, soldered to the BACK of the plate over the 5.0 × 4.0 centre slot, plate LIFTED CLEAR while soldering, fillet inside the slot only — never a ring terminal under the M1.2. Insulation must reach the slot mouth (bare through the wall = short risk on the outboard face). Strain relief is OFF THE PART and LOAD-BEARING (the tie holes + groove of 928dfd4 were dropped in 6053128): far-end slack at the shell anchors → RTV-tacked 15–20 mm service loop on the cap face (outward or tangential from the slot, never inboard — r 9.5 socket sweep; the textured bed face keys the tack) → RTV MOUTH SEAL in the slot (Permatex 81158 — non-acetoxy, safe next to the joint; 24 h cure before insertion. ⚠️ a mouth seal keyed into layer lines, NOT a through-thickness grommet: the operator kept the slot at 5.0 × 4.0, and the recorded ~0.3 mm shrink would leave only ~0.2 mm radial. That shrink figure is DISPUTED — a 2026-10-10 re-measure of the same Ø8.6 nominal read +0.08 OVERSIZE (spool changed 10-07, Bambu Translucent → Inland PETG+); if the new figure holds, the as-printed annulus is ~0.39 radial and the grommet is real with NO geometry change. The plate-15 coupon arbitrates; grommet wording returns only on a coupon-measured ≥ ~0.35 mm radial) → joint carries current only. Acceptance = the tug test on every cured lead, never the bond claim. Coupon first, before any cap print. Cells OUT while soldering. Keep-out on BOTH caps' outboard faces: r 9.5 (measured 13 mm-socket sweep; nut envelope r 7.506 — supersedes the old r ≥ 8.5 link-arc rule; centre-slot leads naturally run at r ≈ 13). ⛔ Dead regimes, dated: 22 AWG / Ø2.0 / 1.2 mm-feeler (2026-10-06); per-pocket rotation + tie-line bearings (928dfd4, same day). The 5222 spring's four-wire at 2.6 A still gates the pack (≤ 30 mΩ → bypass optional; else solder the bypass strand).
  • I²C bus 1 address map (fleet, 2026-10-22): 0x40 HAT INA219 (factory, never reworked) · 0x41 wand PCA9685 (pad A0 bridged) · 0x44 INA226-MAIN (CH1) · 0x45 INA226-BATT (battery lead) · 0x48 TMP102-bay · 0x49 TMP102-wand. Change the board that is DOCUMENTED, never one whose pads you'd have to guess (the 2026-10-17 HAT/PCA flip). Source of truth: Power_Bay §2.1.
  • AC tests are cut at the robot's own rocker or PSU inlet, never the wall, with the power logger running first. Never hand-chatter a live mains connector (operator, 2026-10-22). Every build includes a tug-test of the AC input-side terminations and a fully seated plug — a generic workmanship check.
