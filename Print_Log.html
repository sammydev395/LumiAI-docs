Print Log

One row per printed part, added on the day it comes off the bed, so anyone can tell whether a part in hand predates a design change. Rows are never edited after the fact; a reprint is a new row.

  • Date: the day the print finished (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • Part: the export STL name in cad/exports/stl/ (no folder). Use one row per part, even when several print on one plate.
  • STL sha: the first 16 hex characters of the sha256 of the export that was printed. This is the design version. When a book or one-off prints from a renamed copy, hash the export it came from.
  • Source: the book and plate (X1C 33, H2S 32 @ f7431ee7) or the one-off folder in cad/3dPrint/.
  • Result: ok, failed (didn't finish), or broke (finished, failed in use). Say what happened in Notes.

To find out whether a logged part has changed since it was printed, run python docs/tools/print_log_check.py. It hashes each part's current export and lists every part whose newest row is out of date (CHANGED since printed).
