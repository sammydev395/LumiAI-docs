(Order changed 2026-11-20 at operator request: holes → bolt down → slots → contour.) ~2 min
OP2 — Camera Slots T2 cuts the OAK-D camera clearance slot and the 2nd slot with the plate already held down. Watch. ~1 min
OP3 — Outside Contour T2 cuts the outside perimeter of the plate (91.3 × 51 with tail tabs), tool-radius compensated outward 1.588 mm. Final pass at Z=−3.0 severs the part. Spindle parks X=120, Y=−60, Z=25, M5, M30. Watch the final pass — breakthrough. The surrounding stock comes free; the finished part stays bolted. ~1 min
POST De-burr edges + any flash from the slot. The 4 M4 holes are the camera mount; the 2 M3 holes are the adapter mount. Unscrew the 4 M3 bolt-downs, lift the plate off. Clean the OAK-D M4 holes of any clinging swarf. ~3 min

Total runtime: ~4 min cutting + ~10 min operator time. Allow 20-30 min bench time per plate.

v9.10 heatsink vs this program — differences to keep in mind

  • One tool, not two. No OP1 drill phase. The 1-flute endmill cuts everything — holes, slot, perimeter.
  • One M0, not three. Only the mid-program bolt-down pause; no T1→T2 collet change, no OP2→OP3 waste-removal.
  • No special waste behaviour. OP3 severs cleanly — surrounding stock comes free as one surrounding frame, not 4 corner offcuts. Puck-style corner-offcut hazard does NOT apply here.
  • Plate is thin (2.04 mm) and small. Edge clamps alone will drift under lateral load if the stock isn't a snug clamp — don't skimp on the clamp plan.

Readiness-pass fixes applied to the NC 2026-10-11

The underlying NC had been sitting unchanged since the Ultra plate was cut in March 2026 and failed the current docs/rules/cnc.md audit on four of six counts. Fixes, content-identical in G-code motion:

  • LF → CRLF line endings. UCCNC on LF-only reads the whole file as one line.
  • M6 T2 deleted. Replaced with a dated removal note. On this manual-tool-change UCCNC install the M6 macro consumes the following S..M3 and the spindle never starts.
  • Explicit G21 added to the preamble. File header comment said "mm" but UCCNC needs the actual mode word.
  • M0 #0 setup banner added at program start — gives the operator a confirm-setup pause before any cutting, matching the heatsink and base plate NCs in this repo.

Motion delta vs the Ultra-cut version: +G21, −M6 T2. No X/Y/Z/F coordinates changed. Finished part geometry is unchanged.

import re

    'non-ASCII':      sum(b>127 for b in raw),
    ';-comments':     sum(1 for L in lines if L.lstrip().startswith(';')),
    'unclosed (':     sum(1 for L in lines if L.strip().startswith('(') and not L.strip().endswith(')')),
    '[]-in-()':       sum(1 for L in lines if L.strip().startswith('(') and ('[' in L or ']' in L)),

    'lone-LF':        raw.count(b'\n') - raw.count(b'\r\n'),
    'T# M6':          sum(1 for L in lines if re.match(r'^T\d+\s+M6\s*$', L.strip())),
    'M6 T#':          sum(1 for L in lines if re.match(r'^M6\s+T\d+\s*$', L.strip())),
