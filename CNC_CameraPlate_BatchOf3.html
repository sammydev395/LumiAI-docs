OAK-D camera plate — batch of 3 bench prep (Halo + Nova + spare)

Program: cad/exports/cnc/CameraMount_Oak-D.nc — UNCHANGED, run three times Per-plate reference: docs/CNC_CameraPlate_Single.md — all per-plate mechanics (M0 sequence, hazards, verifier) Rules of engagement: docs/rules/cnc.md

Operator (2026-10-20): "cnc machining is a pain and once we have a plate in a fixture best to machine more since it creates a chip mess and clean up" — cut the three plates in one fixture session: Halo (new), Nova (next build), spare. One tool install, one Z zero, one chip cleanup; XY zero per plate.

Why this doc exists

The NC is written to cut ONE plate centred at the active work zero. Three plates = three runs of the same NC. The win is not modifying the NC — it is leaving the Stepcraft set up (tool, collet, Z zero, spoilboard, vacuum, dust shoe) across all three cuts and only moving the stock in through the workflow once.

Do not generate a modified-coordinates NC. Prior sessions have hit the usual UCCNC syntax traps (lone-LF, nested parens, M6 handling) every time an NC gets rewritten. The original passes the full 8/8 audit; keep it.

PLATE #2 (Nova) Jog to plate #2 scribe (Y_sheet=89.5, +60 mm from plate #1). Re-zero X/Y at the scribe. Same NC. Cycle Start. ~15 min

POST Unbolt the 12 M3 SHCS. Lift 3 finished plates off the spoilboard (plates are held to spoilboard via their bolts, not to each other). Lift the sheet skeleton (held by edge clamps + waste screws). De-burr edges + slot flash on each plate. Vacuum chips. ~10 min

Est. total: ~55-65 min for the three plates. The nesting win is in the amortised setup + the single chip cleanup, not in cutting time.

Z zero across all three plates (one sheet)

One Z zero carries all three — same sheet, same thickness. The OP1 holes cut to Z=−3.0 which is 1 mm into spoilboard, well inside the margin for the 2 mm sheet with ±0.3 mm nominal tolerance.

If the sheet shows visible curl / local lift anywhere (sheet not flat everywhere — no tape now), re-zero Z at that plate's centre specifically before Cycle Start.

XY zero across all three plates

Must re-zero XY between plates — the NC is written around a 0,0 NC-zero and we are physically moving to different NC-zero points on the same sheet. There is no way around this short of modifying the NC with G-code work-offset switches (G54/G55/G56), which we are explicitly not doing.

Scribe each plate's NC-zero point on the sheet BEFORE clamping. Each scribe is 3 mm south (toward the clear-end edge, i.e. the tail-tab side) of its plate's physical centre, because the +6 mm camera lift grew the plate from the camera end only. Touch-off the spindle at each scribe with the spindle lamp.

One-zero program (operator request 2026-11-21)

CameraMount_Oak-D_x3.nc cuts all three plates from one work zero at plate 1's scribe. Plates 2 and 3 are the single-plate moves shifted +60 and +120 mm in Y (verified: each plate's moves equal the single-plate NC exactly). Order: 12 round holes → park X120 Y-60 + M0 (screw all 12 down) → both slots on 3 plates → 3 contours. One Z touch-off. Y extent of the cutter from the zero: −24.06 … +150.05 (sheet Y 5.44 … 179.55 with scribes as before). The other two scribes are check marks. Only change vs the single-plate moves: retracts in the slot/contour ops are Z8 instead of Z5, since 12 screw heads stand proud. Generator: cad/freecad/gen_cameramount_nc_x3.py. Dry-run it above the stock first — it has not been cut. The single-plate NC remains for per-plate runs.

Sheet orientation (operator-confirmed 2026-11-20)

Sheet long side runs front to back. Clean end toward the operator (machine front, Y−); the used end (T-cutout, slot, 2 small holes) at the back (Y+, home side). Each plate's camera end faces the back; the tail tabs face the operator. Machine home is back-left, X+ to the right. Stock: uxcell 6061, 300 × 150 × 2 mm, ≥185 mm clean length.

Mid-program M0 bolt-down per plate

The NC pauses after OP1 (the 4 round holes: 2 camera + 2 tail-tab, drilled first — order changed 2026-11-20) so you can bolt the plate down through them before the slots (OP2) and the part-separating OP3. Per plate: 4× M3 × ~15 mm SHCS, finger-snug into the spoilboard. These bolts go into fresh spoilboard every time — do not reuse an existing hole set for a plate at a different XY zero.

12 bolts total for the batch (4 per plate × 3 plates). After each OP3 completes, those 4 bolts stay in the spoilboard with the stock they are holding; demount at the end of the batch.

Hazards

  • Thin-stock flex after plate 1 severs: the sheet has an increasingly large hole in it after each plate cut, and the 5.89 mm webs are tighter than the pre-lift 10 mm layout. Long-edge clamps + the 6 waste screws keep it rigid (no tape); if the sheet flexes, plate 2 / plate 3 XY zeros drift — re-touch Z at every plate, since flatness varies without tape. Web toe clamps are optional per operator judgment.
  • Single SHARPRO, no spare: 6061 is clean-cutting but still weldable to the flute if the bit dulls. Inspect the flute between plates. STOP at the first stringer or stuck Al.
  • M0 bolt-down must hold through OP3 breakthrough: 4× M3 × ~15 mm SHCS per plate, self-tap into virgin spoilboard. The surrounding sheet is held by clamps + waste screws, not by the plate's bolts.
  • OP3 breakthrough severs the plate from the surrounding sheet: vacuum running, keep hands clear. The plate stays on 4 bolts; the sheet skeleton stays on clamps + waste screws.

Operator standing rule captured here for future CNC work
