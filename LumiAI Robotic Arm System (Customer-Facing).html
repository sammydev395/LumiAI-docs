Core Narrative (One Machine, Many Jobs)

Position LumiAI as a single ceiling-mounted machine that does the work of several devices at once — the same arm, camera, and sensor suite handle multiple jobs simultaneously, and the platform is extensible to more:

  • Light — aims a precise, tracked beam exactly where it's needed, hands-free, from spotlight to RGBW scene.

  • Sense — tracks people, tables, and occupancy in real time with on-device vision that understands the whole room, not just a point.
  • Voice & Gesture — talk to it, point, or just walk in; the iFlytek far-field sound array (mic array + speaker) takes natural spoken commands and talks back, alongside on-device face and gesture recognition.

How It Works (Sense → See → Aim → Light)

A passive sensor wakes the system, the camera understands the scene, the arm aims, and the wand lights — in well under a second, with zero buttons.

  1. Sense presence — a 4-sensor PIR ring gives ~360° coverage in total darkness, waking the arm the instant anyone enters (or on the spoken wake word, at micro-amp standby).
  2. See the scene — the OAK-D S2 stereo-depth AI camera recognizes faces, gestures, and motion on-device (OpenCV + PyTorch on a Raspberry Pi 5).
  3. Aim precisely — six degrees of freedom let the arm track a moving target smoothly: a person, a performer, or your hands on the workbench.
  4. Light it up — the Lumi Wand delivers a clean spotlight today, with 12-channel addressable RGBW scenes, beam shaping, and music sync next.

Three Parts, One Brain

An arm to move, a wand to light, and a sensor suite to perceive — engineered in-house on a Raspberry Pi 5 platform with battery backup.

  • The Arm — LumiAI Arm: a 6-axis chassis with a custom camera mount, machined base plate, and an integrated LiFePO4 UPS. Ceiling- or wall-mounted for full-room reach. (Compute: RPi 5 + 1 TB NVMe · Backup: LiFePO4 UPS)
  • The Wand — Lumi Wand: the business end — a 12-driver addressable RGBW light head with active thermal control, swappable between spotlight, floodlight, and effect attachments. (Control: I²C · PCA9685 · Variants: Arm / Flashlight)
  • The Sensors — Sensor Suite: a perception ring around the base — passive PIR presence that fires in total darkness, plus power, thermal, and air-quality telemetry that corroborate your alarms and feed the AI. (Presence: 4× PIR in the dark · Hazard: CO + smoke + PM · Voice: iFlytek far-field sound array)

🚀 Product Overview

Concept

A 6-DOF robotic arm platform integrating an AI-driven optical camera and a versatile gripper-mounted lighting system. Designed to be modular and environment-adaptive, the product enhances lighting control in garages, kitchens, restaurants, and entertainment venues by intelligently following human gestures, faces, and movement. The system is typically ceiling-mounted (inverted) or side wall-mounted, providing optimal coverage and tracking capabilities from elevated positions.

Key Features

  • AI Vision System:
    • Arm-mounted OAK-D S2 stereo-depth AI camera (standard on every unit) for on-device face, gesture, and depth-aware tracking.
    • PIR sensor for initial presence detection, enabling smart energy usage.
  • Voice System:
    • iFlytek far-field sound array (far-field mic array + speaker) for natural spoken commands, wake-word standby, and voice feedback.
  • Lighting System:
    • Relay-controlled spotlight (first release).
    • Lumi Wand: 12-channel addressable RGBW light module (3× independently controllable 10 W RGBW LEDs) gripped and aimed by the arm.
    • Modular & standalone-capable: the same Lumi Wand can be sold separately as a battery-powered handheld light via the LumiAI Battery Extension (see Standalone Product section).
    • Future versions: RGB light effects, dynamic beam shaping, and synchronized music-light interactions.
    • Flexible gripper mount allows swap-out between spotlight, floodlight, or decorative lighting attachments.
  • Robotic Arm:
    • US-assembled 6-axis robotic arm, finished with custom camera integration, UPS housing, and sensor boards.
    • Mounting Options: Typically installed as ceiling-mounted (inverted) or side wall-mounted for optimal coverage and tracking range.
  • Hardware Platform:
    • Raspberry Pi 5, upgraded with 1 TB NVMe storage.
    • Integrated UPS module for uninterrupted operation.
    • On-device AI models for gesture and face recognition (OpenCV + PyTorch/ONNX on RPi5).
    • Future roadmap: MQTT/REST API integration for home-automation & restaurant lighting systems.

Why LumiAI Is Different

  • Most robotic lighting systems are fixed-mount and pre-programmed (DJ lights, moving-head lights).
  • LumiAI adds true autonomy + human interaction, making it ideal for smart homes, garages, kitchens, gyms, and restaurants/bars seeking interactive ambiance.
  • Scalable from utility (garage task light) to experience (dance floor entertainment) to AI coaching (gym/fitness).
  • Modular product family: one shared wand engine sells two ways — embedded in the robotic arm system and as a standalone battery-powered handheld light — broadening what you can do with a single platform.

🔦 Lumi Wand Anywhere — Standalone (Battery-Powered)

The Lumi Wand is a standalone product in its own right — a battery-powered, app- and touch-controlled RGBW light that goes anywhere: no arm, no tether, no mains, no install. The LumiAI Battery Extension (a modular bottom section) screws onto the exact same wand upper assembly used in the arm system, so you get the Halo arm's premium light engine in a take-anywhere form. Sold standalone as the Lumi Wand Anywhere. Studio-grade color, anywhere. The light that goes where you do.

How It Works — One Wand, Two Products

The wand body is variant-agnostic: it terminates in a standard threaded joint and one 6-pin connector in its end plate, and does not care what is on the other side of it — the arm tether and the battery extension present the same six nets on the same connector.

  • Arm-Tethered SKU → wand body + end plate and retaining ring, powered/controlled through the robotic arm's power bay via the end-plate connector.
  • Standalone SKU → wand body + Battery Extension, fully self-contained and portable.

What's in the Battery Extension

  • 12-Channel Addressable RGBW Light: 3× independently controllable 10 W RGBW LEDs — the same premium light engine as the arm-mounted wand.
  • Touchscreen Control: Integrated 1.9" color touch LCD for on-device color, brightness, and effect selection — no phone or app required to operate.
  • Wireless Connectivity: Built-in Wi-Fi + Bluetooth 5.0 for app control, firmware updates, and future smart-lighting integrations.
  • LiFePO4 Battery + USB-C PD: 4× 26700 LiFePO4 cells (~51 Wh), recharged from any standard USB-C PD charger — no proprietary dock. (Runtime, light output, and IP rating are TBD — bench measurement pending.)
  • Mounts Anywhere: a 1/4"-20 threaded insert on the body screws onto any camera tripod, light stand, or bracket (LCD facing up) — or just hold it. ~1 kg, balanced for any ball/pan head.
  • Rugged, One-Handed Form Factor: illuminated rocker switch and a stepped, grippable silhouette built for field use.
