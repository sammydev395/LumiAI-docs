0. Conventions — read first

  • Tool changes are manual on the StepCraft 840D UCCNC. Every M6 is preceded by a G0 that parks the spindle at an operator-accessible position (off the workpiece, with clearance) and an M0 pause so the operator can change the bit before pressing Cycle Start.
    • Heatsink programs park at X=0, Y=−60 (30 mm in front of the 60 mm plate).
    • Base-plate programs (300×200 or 200×200 sheet) park at X=0, Y=−170 or X=130, Y=0 — 20+ mm clear of the longest edge so the operator has room for the tool.
  • Peck-drill vs. direct drill. We peck-drill (and spot-mark with an end mill first) only when the drill could wander on entry. Rule of thumb:
    • Thick stock, small-diameter drill, precision hole → spot-mark with 1/8" endmill at 0.5 mm first (rule-of-thumb; not currently applied on any part in this guide because §3 heatsink and §5/§6 base plates all use the 135° split-point Milwaukee cobalt drill which self-centres — no spot-mark needed).
    • Thin sheet (≤3 mm), larger drill, non-critical alignment → direct drill, no spot-mark (base plates: 2 mm aluminum, Ø4.0 drills, ±0.2 mm tolerance is plenty). On thin sheet the drill doesn't have enough depth to wander meaningfully.
  • M0 pauses also mark workholding transitions — e.g. the v7 heatsink program pauses after OP2 so the operator can screw the piece down through the just-drilled 4× M2 clearance holes before OP3 (Ø6 wire drill) and OP4 (cut-off, if from bar stock).
  • The LumiAI base plate runs as a SINGLE-setup job (v5). Blank is mounted MOUNT-FACE-UP. T1 (Milwaukee cobalt drill) drills all 22 through-holes (PSU + STRAP + CARRIAGE + POST) at Ø3.175. One M0 pause: bolt the plate down through some drilled holes + swap T1 → T2 (SHARPRO 1/8" O-flute, 1/4" shank — collet change 1/8"→1/4" OZ8) + re-touch Z. Then T2 engraves "LumiAI" and cuts the fence-outline profile completely through. No CNC-time flip. All coordinates are X-mirrored (toolB) so the operator's off-machine install-flip (180° about the long Y axis, engraving-face-down) puts every hole and the engraving at their designed positions. See §6.
  • MM-1000 spindle uses OZ8 collets, not ER11. This guide's collet references are all OZ8 (DIN 6388A / EOC8 / Kress 1050 family, M17×1 nut). Standard OZ8 sizes are sparse — no Ø3.5, Ø4.5, etc. — so drilled parts (servo plate §5, LumiAI base plate §6) use a pilot-drill + hand-drill workflow: a 1/8" cobalt twist drill (Milwaukee or YG-1, both 1/8" shank, 1/8" OZ8) peck-drills through-pilots at every drill position on the CNC, then the operator hand-drills each pilot to spec Ø off-machine with a normal HSS-Co drill set. The pilot guarantees centre (the drill can't wander). On-machine tooling for the whole guide: only 1/8" + 1/4" OZ8 collets — both already on hand. No OZ8 drill collets in odd sizes needed.
  • Use a real twist drill for through-drilling, NOT a small endmill. Endmills aren't designed for axial plunging — the centre is a near-zero-velocity zone where chips smear and aluminum welds to the flute, snapping the tool. The 2 mm SainSmart Nano Blue Coat was used for pilots through 2026-06-22 and broke on hole #1 of the servo plate run on 2026-06-23 at F100 plunge. Replaced with a 1/8" Milwaukee cobalt twist drill (Home Depot, in hand) which now drills all the way THROUGH the stock on-machine (Z−3.0 for 2 mm servo plate, Z−4.0 for 3 mm LumiAI base plate). Off-machine hand-drilling now just enlarges to spec Ø. The SainSmart small endmills stay in the kit for lateral-cut features — the servo hex pocket (§5 OP2) uses the 2.1 mm from the 10-pack (the 2.0 mm broken in the v1 run; 2.1 mm is the user-confirmed in-hand backup).

Workholding & Workflow

Single setup, one tool change, no flip. Hold-down: edge toe clamps for OP1 (no tape), then through-bolts (POST.5–POST.8, listed in the NC's tool-change banner) for OP2 engrave + OP3 profile.

  1. Fixturing: clamp the 200.6 × 302 × 3 mm blank to a sacrificial spoilboard MOUNT FACE UP (the face that will be engraved — becomes the plate's underside after install). OP1 cuts 1.0 mm into the spoilboard at Z−4.00; OP3 cuts 0.2 mm into it at Z−3.20. Keep clamps clear of the fence-outline path. Home all axes; set X/Y zero at the stock centre and Z zero on the top face with T1 (Milwaukee 1/8" cobalt twist drill, 1/8" OZ8 collet, stick-out 12-15 mm — see §1 / PRE-3).
  2. OP1: T1 drills all 22 holes THROUGH stock (2 PSU + 4 STRAP + 8 CARRIAGE + 8 POST; G83, Z−4.00, R=2.0, Q=1.0, F60 plunge). All come out clean Ø3.175 cylindrical through-holes (drill point intrudes 1.0 mm into spoilboard, full Ø reaches stock bottom). Coordinates are toolB (X-mirrored) — see the "How to cross-check" note under Hole Positions. Hand-enlarge to spec Ø off-machine after the CNC job ends.
  3. 🔧 M0 (only one): spindle parks X=0, Y=−170, Z=15. Three actions: (a) drive M3 SHCS through some of the just-drilled Ø3.175 holes (the four end POST pilots POST.5–POST.8 — 4 bolts, spread over the plate length) into the spoilboard for positive hold-down through OP2 + OP3; (b) swap T1 → T2 (SHARPRO 1/8" O-flute, 1/4" shank), collet 1/8" OZ8 → 1/4" OZ8; (c) re-touch Z=0 on the new bit. Cycle Start. T2 STAYS for the rest of the job. No plate flip.
  4. OP2: engrave "LumiAI" into the mount face (0.75 mm zigzag pocket). Model pre-mirror + toolB cancel so the text reads normal in the viewer; the install-flip is what puts it on the plate's underside reading correctly from outside.
  5. OP3 (last cut): the fence outline is profiled completely through (Z−3.20), tool radius pre-compensated outward, no tabs. The part stays held by the through-bolts; the outer waste frame is severed and comes free — keep clear on the final pass, then remove the frame and unbolt the finished part.
  6. OFF-MACHINE — hand-enlarge the 22 drilled-through holes to spec Ø. Power drill or drill press, HSS-Co bits: 2 PSU → Ø4.0; 4 STRAP → Ø4.5; 8 CARRIAGE → Ø3.5 (close enough to the Ø3.4 model spec); 8 POST → Ø4.5 (or Ø4.2 for tighter M4 fit). The holes are already through at Ø3.175, so bits follow them — no wander.
  7. Install (off-machine, once): physically flip the finished plate 180° about its long (Y) axis so the engraved face goes DOWN. Every hole lands at its designed position because toolB pre-compensated for the flip.

Total: 2 on-machine tools, 1 tool change, no FLIP, ~40-50 min CNC time + ~10 min hand-enlarge. From v3's "4 tools + 3 tool changes + 1 FLIP" and v4's "3 tools + 2 tool changes + 1 FLIP".
