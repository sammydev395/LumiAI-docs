OP2 Ø54 outside contour, 10 passes at 1 mm DOC down to Z=−10.05. Cutter orbits at R=28.5875 (R27.0 + 1.5875). S18000, F300 XY, F100 plunge. Spindle parks X=0, Y=−60, Z=25, M5. ⚠️ Cutter outer sweep is 30.175 mm vs. bar half-width 30 — orbit clips the bar's ±Y edges at ±3.24 from puck X (two cusps per side). From the BAR (unlike a 60 × 60 blank), there are NO 4 loose corner offcuts — bar stays structurally continuous between pucks and to the clamps. Watch for chatter at fin peaks (thin standing wall interface); F override 50% in UCCNC if chatter appears. Air blast on, puck stays bolted. No spare SHARPRO — if the bit shows chip-weld or dulling signs mid-pass, STOP at the next pass end. ~8 min
M0 #2 — Clear chip packing from fin valleys around the orbit (brass pick, air blast — chips get trapped between fins). Puck is the only loose piece and is held by 3 body bolts — DO NOT unscrew bolts, DO NOT change the tool, DO NOT re-zero. Inspect T2 flute for Al weld; brass-pick + IPA if needed. Press Cycle Start. ~3 min
OP3 Keyway slot cut into the isolated puck rim. 10 passes at 1 mm DOC down to Z=−10.05. Per pass: plunge in air at (X=28.588, Y=+0.6125), G1 X=26.388 (cut +Y wall), retract Z, reposition to Y=−0.6125, plunge, G1 X=26.388 (cut −Y wall), retract. Slot: 4.4 wide × 2.2 deep × 10 through. Spindle parks X=0, Y=−60, Z=25, M5, M30. Watch the final pass — breakthrough. ~3 min
POST De-burr Ø54 rim + keyway slot edges with fine file or 400-grit. ~2 min. Finish, unscrew body bolts, lift puck off fixture. ~5 min

Total runtime: ~10 min cutting + ~15 min operator (M0 pauses + setup + post). Allow 30-40 min bench time per puck.

v9.10 hazards (what's new vs the Ø48 cut you ran before)

  1. 4 loose corner offcuts at OP2 breakthrough instead of a continuous waste ring. Tape + vacuum through pass 10. Clear at M0 #2 before OP3 touches the puck.
  2. Slot is a separate OP3, not an inline per-pass detour. Previous Ø48 integrated-detour version broke a bit; this one plunges in air, cuts one wall, retracts, repositions, cuts the other wall. (v9.9 split 2026-07-17, v9.9.1 retract-between-walls 2026-07-18.)
  3. Keyway radial position moved OUT 3 mm with the rim. Slot DIMS are unchanged (4.4 × 2.2 × 10) and it still mates L_TAB_KEY_TANG=4.0 / L_TAB_KEY_RADIAL=2.0 at the 30° L-tab.
  4. NC header carries 3 old Dia54 typos that meant Dia48 — corrected 2026-10-11 in the same commit as this doc.
